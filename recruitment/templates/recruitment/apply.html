Before you submit: who we look for

Most of our members joined with zero engineering projects behind them. We don't expect experience. We expect curiosity, reliability and enthusiasm.

A bad answer looks like: "I want to join because it will look good on my CV."

A good answer looks like: "I've never built anything before, but I've always wanted to know how a fuel cell actually works, and I'll show up every week to find out."

If that second one sounds like you, you're exactly who we want. Give your answers one last read, then send it in.
