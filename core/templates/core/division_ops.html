Operations Division

Our Operations Division is the backbone of HFR. They deliver the essential maintenance, organisation and creative direction of the society.

Business

This team handles the operation of the society, from providing a range of events, lecture talks and worksite trips. They communicate with the University, the SRC and our range of diverse sponsors.

Finance & Contracts

This team handles the society's books and balances. They ensure we strategically fund our projects to give opportunity to as many University of Glasgow students as possible.

Creative Direction

This team handles the creative direction and society identity. They are the creative minds that script, direct and film promotional videos. They create posters and aesthetic posts, and they build the identity of who HFR members are.
