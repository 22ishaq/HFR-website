Our Journey

From Zero to Monaco

The milestones, people, and ambition shaping UG Hydrogen Fuel Racing.

UGEV ESTABLISHED

On December 2023 ‘University of Glasgow Eco Vehicle’ society was created.

VERSION 1

UGEV began the development for a vehicle to compete in the Shell Eco Marathon.

H.F.R FORMED

UGEV became Hydrogen Fuel Racing during September 2024.

H.S.N

HFR is asked to assist the Hydrogen Scotland Network at the 12th annual Monaco Energy Boat Challenge.

EXPANSION

Projects involving Aeronautical and Nautical form two new Air and Sea Divisions.

COMPETITION

HFR has its first official entry into a competition, the 13th annual Monaco Energy Boat Challenge.

CONSTRUCTION

The construction of the Shell Eco Marathon begins.

FUTURE

Three divisions, one mission. The next chapter is being written right now, and you could be part of it.
